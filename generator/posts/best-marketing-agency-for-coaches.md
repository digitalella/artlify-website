Your coaching business needs suitable enquiries and a clear path to enrolment. A proposal centred on posting volume can leave you unsure how the work supports that goal.

This guide compares seven agencies you can investigate, including broad-service providers and agencies with paid-media or social specialisms. Inclusion does not establish that an agency has verified experience with your type of coaching.

**Disclosure: Artlify is our agency.** We have included it with the same emphasis on fit, evidence and questions to ask. The list is not ordered by quality or presented as an independent award.

## How did we choose these marketing agencies?

We considered published service relevance, clarity of scope and the questions a coach would need answered before hiring. We reviewed official agency pages rather than assigning invented ratings or repeating unverified client results.

Our comparison criteria are:

- Content and message development.
- Paid acquisition or social media support.
- Landing pages and conversion work.
- Follow-up or email capability where stated.
- Pricing transparency or the need for a scoped quote.
- Evidence a prospective client should request.

The descriptions reflect information checked in November 2026, and we update this list each year.

This review does not verify client satisfaction, current availability or coaching-sector outcomes. Those remain questions for your own selection process.

## Is LYFE Marketing worth considering for social media support?

Consider LYFE Marketing if your brief centres on social media and you want to investigate how organic content and advertising would be coordinated. Its official service pages describe social media marketing and social advertising. Official site: [LYFE Marketing](https://www.lyfemarketing.com/).

Ask for a coaching-relevant proposal showing the role of educational content, offer messaging and calls to action. Clarify whether filming, editing, lead handling and landing pages are included.

Do not assume that a social package includes the full enrolment journey. Ask which outcomes will be reported and how your sales information will inform decisions.

Request current pricing directly rather than relying on a price copied into an old comparison article.

## Is WebFX worth considering for a broader marketing brief?

Consider WebFX if you want to investigate a scope spanning several digital channels. Its official pages list services including SEO, paid advertising, social media, content, email and conversion work. Official site: [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/).

Ask the team to explain which services your coaching business needs now and which can wait. A broad menu does not mean you should buy every service.

Request examples involving a similar offer, sales process and budget. Clarify who will coordinate delivery and what your own team must contribute.

WebFX publishes a pricing page, but your decision should use a current written proposal for your scope. Official source: [WebFX packages](https://www.webfx.com/digital-marketing/services/packages/).

## Is Ignite Visibility worth considering for multi-channel work?

Consider Ignite Visibility if your brief involves coordinating several digital activities and you want to assess its approach to your coaching offer. Its official website describes a range of digital marketing services. Official site: [Ignite Visibility](https://www.ignitevisibility.com/).

Ask how the team would connect each recommended channel to an agreed objective. Request a clear explanation of the website, content and sales-process dependencies.

Your discovery call should establish who owns qualification and follow-up. Ask whether the agency will measure applications, booked calls, attendance or completed enrolments, and what information it needs from you.

If it proposes several channels, request priorities and the reason for their order. Avoid accepting channel coverage as an objective in itself.

Ask for examples relevant to a coaching business rather than assuming that general service-business experience covers your programme, audience and sales process.

## Is KlientBoost worth considering for paid lead generation?

Consider KlientBoost if you want to investigate paid acquisition alongside landing-page and conversion work. Its official PPC service page describes paid search, paid social and conversion support. Official source: [KlientBoost PPC agency](https://klientboost.com/ppc-agency/).

Check creative production, landing-page ownership and the total budget before assessing affordability.

## What should you ask before choosing any agency?

Ask how the agency will connect its work to your definition of a suitable prospective client. Then compare scope, evidence, costs and responsibilities using the same brief.

Follow these steps:

1. Describe your offer and ideal client.
2. Define who should and should not apply.
3. Share your current sales process and constraints.
4. Request relevant work with verified outcomes.
5. Compare the full cost and required inputs.
6. Review access, ownership and cancellation terms.

Keep your shortlist focused on fit. If the agency cannot explain how its work relates to your goal, ask for clarification before progressing. For a wider selection framework, read [How to Choose a Marketing Agency](/blog/how-to-choose-a-marketing-agency/).
