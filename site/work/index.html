Bella Books

E-commerce bookstore

The challenge
Sales were stalling. The brand had limited visibility, ads that weren't converting, a WooCommerce store with weak tracking, generic offers and an underused email list.
What we did
We repositioned the brand, restructured offers and pricing, moved the store to Shopify with Meta Pixel tracking and set up Omnisend email. Then we ran Meta ad campaigns, brand campaigns and a full email system.
