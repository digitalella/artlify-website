How Much Does a Marketing Agency Cost in 2027?

By Emmanuella Omeh·Published 15 November 2026·5 min read
Hiring
Quick answer

Marketing agency cost depends on the services, workload and responsibilities in your agreement. Artlify's fees typically range from $500 to $10,000 (USD), depending on scope. Compare the full commitment, including advertising, production and software, and request a written scope before deciding whether a quote fits your budget.

A marketing quote only becomes useful when you know what it buys. “Social media management” could mean publishing supplied posts, or it could include strategy, copy, design, editing, community management and reporting.

The same applies to advertising. Does the agency only manage campaigns, or does it also produce creatives, improve your landing page and write the follow-up emails?

This guide helps coaches and e-commerce founders compare agency fees without treating different services as the same purchase. It also explains what Artlify has confirmed about pricing and what still needs a written quote.

How much does Artlify charge?

Artlify's fees typically range from $500 to $10,000 (USD), depending on scope. Depending on the engagement, that's quoted as a monthly fee or as a total for the agreed work.

Artlify offers one-off projects, monthly retainers for a single service, or a combination of services. Retainers have a three-month minimum, with one month's cancellation notice after that.

Avoid assuming that the bottom of the range includes every service. Ask for a proposal that identifies your priorities, the deliverables included and the work your team will still handle.

For a coach, that discussion might cover content, lead generation and nurturing. For an online store, it might cover product messaging, ads and the purchase journey. Your quote should reflect the actual assignment.

What makes one marketing proposal cost more than another?

Compare the workload, specialist responsibilities and delivery requirements in each proposal. Ask the agency to explain the cost of the scope you are requesting rather than presenting a fee without context.

Useful questions include:

  • How many channels are included?
  • Who develops the strategy and writes the copy?
  • Who supplies photography and raw video?
  • Does the agency build or edit landing pages?
  • Are email sequences included?
  • Who manages comments and direct messages?
  • What reporting and review conversations are provided?

Also discuss the number of offers, products, markets and decision-makers involved. Ask whether those requirements are included or treated as additional work.

A quote for managing one clearly defined offer should be assessed against that brief. It should not be compared casually with a proposal covering an entire product catalogue and several customer journeys.

What costs should you separate from the agency fee?

Request separate lines for management, media, production, software and any setup work. This gives you a clearer view of what you must fund to carry out the plan.

Use this comparison when reviewing a proposal:

Cost category What to clarify Question to ask
Agency fee Strategy and ongoing delivery What work does this cover?
Advertising Money allocated to media Who pays it and where?
Production Photography, filming and editing What must we supply?
Software Email, scheduling or other tools Whose subscription is used?
Setup Initial builds and configuration Is this included or separate?
Additional work Tasks outside the agreement How is a change approved?

At Artlify, ad spend isn't part of our fee: you pay Meta directly from your own card, and the ad account stays in your name.

Ask for costs in writing. You should be able to distinguish what is confirmed, what is optional and what is only an estimate.

How should you compare quotes in dollars and pounds?

Compare quotes in one currency, using the exchange rate on the day you compare, and keep each supplier's original figure next to it. Artlify quotes in US dollars.

If you pay from a UK or other non-US account, ask your bank or payment provider what conversion fees apply. Ask each agency how long its proposal remains valid and how currency changes are handled during the contract. These questions are particularly useful when your sales income and marketing payments use different currencies.

How do you decide what your business can afford?

Start with the amount your business can sustain across the agreed contract and the work needed to support it. Treat the agency fee as one part of the commitment.

Build your decision in this order:

  1. Define the business problem you want addressed.
  2. Identify the services needed to address it.
  3. List all confirmed and possible costs.
  4. Check cash available across the minimum term.
  5. Decide what evidence you will review before increasing investment.

For an e-commerce business, bring product costs, delivery costs and returns information to the discussion. For a coach, bring programme pricing, delivery capacity and the sales process.

Do not agree to a plan that leaves you unable to fulfil orders, deliver your programme or fund essential operations. Ask for a narrower scope if that is what your current position supports.

Is a cheaper agency always better value?

A lower fee is useful when the scope meets your needs and the total commitment is manageable. Judge value by the responsibilities covered, the quality of evidence and the work you will still need to manage.

Compare these two arrangements:

  • One proposal covers publishing, while your team supplies all finished content.
  • Another covers planning, writing, design and publishing, while your team supplies raw assets and approvals.

Either could be appropriate. The decision depends on your internal capacity.

Ask yourself whether the agency fills a genuine gap. If your team already handles design well, buying more design may not address the problem. If no one owns the funnel, a large posting package may leave your main concern unresolved.

What does an integrated marketing scope look like?

An integrated scope connects the activities involved in moving someone from discovery to a buying decision. Ask the agency to show which parts it will own and how they fit together.

Coach of Success approached Artlify with difficulty attracting qualified leads and a social media presence that needed optimisation. Our work included content strategy, content pillars and a funnel covering ad creative direction, a sales landing page, nurturing and email copy.

She went on to land her first five clients for her $5,000 coaching programme. Not every business needs that full scope, but it shows how the pieces connect.

When reviewing your own proposal, look for a similarly clear explanation of the problem and the work proposed. Ask what the agency will leave to your team.

What should you check before paying a retainer?

Check the scope, payment terms, minimum commitment, cancellation process and handover arrangements. Resolve unclear wording before making the first payment.

For Artlify, the confirmed minimum term is three months. The notice period is one month, subject to that term. Clients receive a complete folder of marketing materials and the marketing strategy document during handover.

You also keep your ad accounts, pages and all the content created for your brand.

Your final review should answer three practical questions: What are we buying? What must we contribute? What happens when the agreement ends? If any answer remains unclear, request a revised proposal.

Frequently asked questions

How does Artlify price its services?

Artlify offers one-off projects and monthly retainers for one service or several. Fees typically range from $500 to $10,000 (USD), quoted monthly or as a total for the agreed work. Request a proposal specifying the exact fee, deliverables and separately funded expenses for your business.

Is ad spend included in the agency fee?

No. At Artlify, you pay Meta directly from your own card, and the ad account stays in your name. Whichever agency you hire, your proposal should state whether media is included, who funds the advertising account and what happens when an increase in campaign spend is proposed.

What is Artlify's minimum contract length?

Artlify's minimum contract length is three months. Cancellation requires one month's notice, subject to that minimum commitment. Review the start date, notice process and final billing arrangements in your written agreement so you understand the financial commitment before work begins.

Can I hire Artlify for only one service?

Yes. You can hire Artlify for a single service on a monthly retainer, a combination of services, or a one-off project. Explain the responsibility you need covered so the proposal can define a suitable scope and the input required from your team.
