Meta Ads for Beginners: How to Plan Your First Facebook & Instagram Campaign

By the Artlify Team·13 November 2026·6 min read
Paid Ads

Meta ads — sponsored posts on Facebook and Instagram — are one of the fastest ways to reach new customers. They are also one of the fastest ways to waste money if you skip the planning. Here is what to get right before you launch your first campaign.

1. Start with one clear objective

Decide what success looks like: website traffic, leads, messages, sales or waitlist signups. Choose the matching campaign objective in Ads Manager, because Meta optimises delivery for the action you select.

2. Set up tracking first

Install the Meta Pixel and Conversions API on your website so you can see which ads lead to signups and purchases. Without tracking, you are guessing. Always compare platform-reported results with your real sales data.

3. Define your audience

Start broad enough for Meta's algorithm to learn, but relevant to your offer. Use location, age and interests as a starting point, and build custom audiences from website visitors, engaged followers and email lists for retargeting.

4. Creative does the heavy lifting

  • Hook attention in the first three seconds.
  • Speak to one problem and one outcome.
  • Show your product or face — people trust people.
  • Test several creatives, not just one.

5. Set a realistic budget

Give each campaign enough budget and time to exit the learning phase before judging results. Changing everything after a day or two resets learning and makes results harder to read.

6. Read the right numbers

Focus on cost per result, click-through rate, conversion rate and, most importantly, your actual cost per sale or client. Scale what works gradually and pause what does not.
