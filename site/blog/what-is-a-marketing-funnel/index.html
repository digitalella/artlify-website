What Is a Marketing Funnel? A Plain-English Guide for Coaches and Small Brands

By the Artlify Team·13 November 2026·5 min read
Funnels

A marketing funnel is the journey someone takes from first discovering your brand to becoming a paying customer. It is called a funnel because many people enter at the top and fewer move through each stage — and your job is to guide the right people forward.

The four stages of a funnel

  • Awareness: people discover you through social media, ads, referrals or search.
  • Interest: they engage with your content, visit your website or download a free resource.
  • Decision: they compare options, book a call or add to cart.
  • Action: they buy, sign up or become a client.

What a simple funnel looks like

For a coach, a simple funnel might be: an Instagram post or ad → a landing page with a free guide → an email sequence that builds trust → an invitation to book a discovery call. For an e-commerce brand, it could be: a product ad → a product page → an abandoned-cart email → a purchase.

The building blocks

  • A traffic source — organic content, paid ads or partnerships.
  • A landing page with one clear goal.
  • A lead magnet or offer that is worth giving an email for.
  • A follow-up sequence that nurtures leads automatically.
  • A booking page or checkout that makes the next step easy.

Common funnel mistakes

The most common mistakes are sending traffic to a homepage instead of a focused landing page, asking for too much too soon, and forgetting to follow up. Most leads are not ready to buy the first time they find you, which is why automation matters.

How to know if your funnel is working

Track conversion rates at each step: how many visitors opt in, how many leads book a call, how many calls become clients. When you know where people drop off, you know exactly what to improve.
