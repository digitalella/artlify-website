What does Artlify do?

Artlify is a full-service marketing agency. We help coaches and growing brands with marketing strategy, social media management, content strategy and creation, copywriting, campaigns, paid ads, influencer and sponsorship marketing, funnel building and marketing automation.

Who do you work with?

We work with coaches, consultants, course creators, e-commerce brands and growing service businesses. Our approach is personalised, so we tailor every engagement to the brand in front of us.

Do you offer one-off projects or only retainers?

Both. We take on one-off projects, such as a funnel build, a launch campaign or a content strategy, and monthly retainers for one service or several. Retainers have a three-month minimum.

How much do your marketing services cost?

Our fees typically range from $500 to $10,000 (USD), depending on the services, platforms and volume of work. Depending on the engagement, we quote a monthly fee or a total for the agreed work. Share your goals through our enquiry form and we'll recommend the right mix and send a tailored proposal.

Is ad spend included in your fees?

No. You pay Meta and other platforms directly from your own card, and the ad accounts stay in your name. Our fee covers strategy, creative, setup, management and reporting.
