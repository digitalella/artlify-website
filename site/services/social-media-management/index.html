Overview

FAQ

Social Media questions.

Ask Us Anything →
Which platforms do you manage?

We manage Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest and X. We will recommend the platforms that make most sense for your audience rather than asking you to be everywhere.

Do you create the content too?

Yes. Social media management can be paired with our content creation service, or we can work with content your team already produces.

How often will you post?

Posting frequency is agreed during strategy and depends on your goals, platform and budget. Consistency matters more than volume.
